# Documentary Credit LC-2026-0142

## Parties

The issuing bank is Vltava Trade Bank a.s., Prague. The applicant is Moravia Precision Components s.r.o., Brno. The beneficiary is Shenzhen Kaida Motion Co., Ltd., Shenzhen. The advising bank is Pearl River Commercial Bank, Shenzhen branch.

## Amount and tolerance

The credit amount is USD 1,250,000.00. A tolerance of plus or minus 5% applies to the credit amount and to the quantity of goods.

## Form, availability and rules

This is an irrevocable documentary credit available with the advising bank by payment at sight. The credit is subject to UCP 600 and to eUCP Version 2.1, so documents may be presented electronically, on paper, or as a mix of both.

## Dates

The credit was issued on 2026-06-15. The latest shipment date is 2026-10-31. The credit expires on 2026-11-30 at the counters of the advising bank. The presentation period is 21 calendar days after the date of shipment, but in any case not later than the expiry date of the credit.

## Shipment

Partial shipments are allowed. Transhipment is not allowed. Shipment is from the port of Shenzhen (Yantian) to the port of Hamburg, with onward delivery to Brno. The Incoterms rule is CIF Hamburg (Incoterms 2020).

## Goods

The goods are 400 units of servo motors model KD-SM750 and 400 units of matching servo drives model KD-SD750, as per pro forma invoice PI-KD-2026-077.

## Documents required

1. Signed commercial invoice in three originals, showing the LC number.
2. Full set of clean on-board ocean bills of lading made out to the order of the issuing bank, marked freight prepaid, notify the applicant. An electronic bill of lading issued on an approved platform is acceptable.
3. Insurance certificate covering 110% of the CIF value, all risks, claims payable in Czech Republic.
4. Packing list in two copies.
5. Certificate of origin issued by a chamber of commerce in China.

## Charges

All banking charges outside the Czech Republic are for the account of the beneficiary.

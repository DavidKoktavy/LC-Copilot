# Documentary Credit LC-2026-0187

## Parties

The issuing bank is Vltava Trade Bank a.s., Prague. The applicant is Bohemia Glassworks a.s., Karlovy Vary. The beneficiary is Lombardia Forni S.p.A., Bergamo. The advising and confirming bank is Banca Alpina S.p.A., Milan.

## Amount and tolerance

The credit amount is EUR 480,000.00. No tolerance applies; the amount is a maximum.

## Form, availability and rules

This is an irrevocable confirmed documentary credit available with the confirming bank by deferred payment at 90 days after the date of shipment. The credit is subject to UCP 600. Paper presentation only; eUCP does not apply.

## Dates

The credit was issued on 2026-08-02. The latest shipment date is 2026-11-15. The credit expires on 2026-12-10 in Milan. The presentation period is 15 calendar days after the date of shipment.

## Shipment

Partial shipments are prohibited. Transhipment is allowed. Shipment is by road from Bergamo to Karlovy Vary under the Incoterms rule FCA Bergamo (Incoterms 2020).

## Goods

One industrial glass melting furnace, model LF-Electra 12, complete with control cabinet, as per contract BG-LF-2026-03.

## Documents required

1. Signed commercial invoice in two originals.
2. CMR road consignment note showing the applicant as consignee.
3. Certificate of origin issued by the Bergamo chamber of commerce.
4. Inspection certificate issued by an independent surveyor confirming conformity with contract BG-LF-2026-03.

## Confirmation charges

Confirmation charges are for the account of the beneficiary.

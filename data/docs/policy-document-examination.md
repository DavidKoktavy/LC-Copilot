# Policy TO-07 — Document Examination and Discrepancies

## Scope

This internal policy of Vltava Trade Bank applies to every presentation received under a documentary credit issued or confirmed by the bank. It supplements, and never overrides, the rules the credit is subject to.

## Examination period

The bank has a maximum of five banking days following the day of presentation to decide whether a presentation is complying. Examiners should aim to finish within three banking days so that a supervisor review fits inside the deadline.

## Examination standard

Data in a document must not conflict with data in the same document, in any other stipulated document, or in the credit. Data does not have to be identical, only consistent. Spelling or typing errors that do not change the meaning of a word are not discrepancies.

## Discrepancy handling

When a presentation is discrepant, the examiner records each discrepancy with a standard code in the trade system. The bank may approach the applicant for a waiver of the discrepancies, but approaching the applicant does not extend the five-banking-day examination period.

## Waivers

A waiver must be received in writing from the applicant through an authenticated channel. A waiver accepted after the bank has sent a refusal notice does not cancel the refusal automatically; the examiner must update the refusal status and inform the presenter.

## Refusal notice

If the bank decides to refuse, it must send a single notice of refusal to the presenter stating every discrepancy and stating what the bank is doing with the documents (holding them pending further instructions, holding them until a waiver is received, returning them, or acting on earlier instructions from the presenter). The notice must be sent no later than the close of the fifth banking day following presentation.

## Four-eyes approval

Every refusal notice must be approved by a second authorised signatory before it is sent. Refusal notices drafted with automated assistance count as drafts until approved by a human signatory.

## Escalation thresholds

Any discrepant presentation above USD 1,000,000 or its equivalent must be escalated to the Head of Trade Operations on the same banking day the discrepancy is identified.

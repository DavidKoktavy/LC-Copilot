# Examiner Notes — Presentation PRS-0142-01

## Presentation summary

Presentation PRS-0142-01 was received on 2026-09-18 under credit LC-2026-0142 for a drawing of USD 812,400.00 covering the first partial shipment of 260 servo motors and 260 servo drives. The presentation was mixed: the bill of lading was electronic and the other documents were on paper.

## Findings

The examiner identified three discrepancies. First, the insurance certificate covers only 100% of the CIF value instead of the required 110%. Second, the commercial invoice does not show the LC number. Third, the electronic bill of lading was presented on a platform that is not on the approved-platform list.

## Escalation

The drawing is below the USD 1,000,000 escalation threshold, so escalation to the Head of Trade Operations is not mandatory. The examiner nevertheless flagged the unapproved platform to the digital trade team.

## Applicant contact

The applicant was contacted on 2026-09-21 and indicated it is willing to waive the invoice discrepancy but not the insurance discrepancy. No written waiver has been received yet.

## Status

The presentation is marked discrepant in the trade system. No refusal notice has been sent.

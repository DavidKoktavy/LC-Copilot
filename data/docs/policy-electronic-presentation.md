# Policy TO-11 — Electronic Presentations under eUCP

## Purpose

This policy explains how Vltava Trade Bank receives and examines electronic records presented under credits that incorporate eUCP Version 2.1. It is an internal guidance note written in plain language and is not a restatement of the ICC rules.

## When electronic presentation is possible

Electronic records may only be presented when the credit expressly states that it is subject to eUCP. If the credit is subject to UCP 600 alone, the bank accepts paper documents only.

## Place for presentation

For electronic records the credit must name the system or address to which records are presented. Records sent to any other channel are treated as not received.

## Notice of completeness

The presenter must give the bank a notice stating that the presentation is complete. The examination period for the electronic part of a presentation starts on the banking day after that notice is received, not on the day the first record arrives.

## Authentication

An electronic record must be capable of being authenticated, meaning the bank can verify the identity of the sender and the source of the data, and confirm that the record has not been altered. Records that fail authentication are treated as not presented.

## Electronic bills of lading and transferable records

The bank accepts electronic bills of lading only from platforms on the approved-platform list maintained by Trade Operations. Under legal frameworks modelled on the UNCITRAL Model Law on Electronic Transferable Records (MLETR), control of an electronic transferable record replaces physical possession of the paper original; the examiner must check that the bank or its nominee is shown as the party in control.

## Corruption of records

If an electronic record appears corrupted, the bank may ask the presenter to re-present it. The examination period is suspended until the record is re-presented, and if it is not re-presented within 30 calendar days the record is treated as not presented.
